Slide 3 | Certification Pathways

What Certificate Should a BESS Supplier Seek? (Section 3)

1. Product Design (PDA)

ABS Product Design Assessment

  • Technical review of defined product and intended service against stated rules.
  • Verification of cell test reports (IEC 62619, IEC 62620, UN 38.3).
  • Verification of thermal propagation evidence (UL 9540A).
  • Validity: 5 Years.

2. Manufacturing (MA)

ABS Manufacturing Assessment

  • Assessment of factory's production and quality control processes.
  • Applies to Clean Electric (BMS/modules) and VFCPL (pack assembly).
  • Requires ISO 9001 quality system basis.
  • Audits: Annual plant surveillance audits.

3. Unit Certification

ABS Type Approval & Unit Cert

  • ABS Type Approval: PDA + MA combined.
  • Unit Certification: Testing the physical assembly at manufacturer with an ABS surveyor where rules require it.
  • Generic product assessment does not settle every installation condition.
OEM vs. Secondary Manufacturing Distinction (from Section 3)
If your company imports cells and builds modules/racks in India, identify who owns the design, who controls changes, and where final manufacture occurs. ABS PDA application distinguishes OEM design ownership from subcontracted secondary manufacturing sites and calls for duplicate PDA arrangements where applicable.

Slide 5 | Thermal Runaway Evidence

Thermal-Runaway Evidence: The 7 Review Questions (Section 8)

The 7 Review Questions to Make Reports Usable

  • 1. Cell Authenticity: Does the tested cell have exactly the same construction as the purchased cell?
  • 2. Test Conditions: Does the report identify SOC (100%), conditioning, initiating method, and orientation?
  • 3. Worst-Case Setup: Was the tested assembly representative of the most challenging offered arrangement?
  • 4. Active Systems: Were cooling and active protective systems running, failed, or unavailable?
  • 5. Propagation Containment: What happened to adjacent cells/modules, enclosure pressure, and gas release?
  • 6. Gas & Heat Data: Are measured gas/heat data sufficient for integrator's ventilation & fire analysis?
  • 7. Configuration Limits: Which changes in spacing, barriers, enclosure, firmware, or coolant invalidate inference?

Class Propagation Rules & Immersion Physics

  • ABS Section 2/2.1 Mandate: Prevent cell-to-cell propagation, or at minimum module-to-module propagation.
  • IRS CN Rev.1 App.1(b)#5: Non-propagation test at 100% SoC without external flaming.
  • UL 9540 vs. UL 9540A: UL distinguishes UL 9540A test method from UL 9540 complete-system certification criteria. A report from one does not justify claiming certification to the other.
  • VFCPL Direct Immersion: Dielectric fluid continuously absorbs sensible heat and suppresses hot-spot formation, halting thermal runaway propagation.

Dielectric Coolant Fluid: Electrical, Fire & Environmental Properties

Electrical Insulation

  • Dielectric Strength: > 50 kV (IEC 60156 / ASTM D877).
  • Volume Resistivity: > 1012 Ω·m at 25°C.
  • Safety Margin: Prevents live busbar arcing up to 1000 VDC even during dynamic vessel motion.

Thermal & Fire Safety

  • Flash Point: > 160°C (ISO 2719).
  • Fire Point: > 190°C (ISO 2592).
  • Kinematic Viscosity: < 35 mm²/s at 40°C; < 3000 mm²/s at -20°C (ASTM D7042).
  • Heat Capacity: High specific heat for thermal buffering.

Environmental & Marine Safety

  • Biodegradability: Readily biodegradable (>60% in 28 days, OECD 301B).
  • Aquatic Ecotoxicity: Non-toxic to marine organisms (OECD 201, 202, 203).
  • Material Compatibility: Zero chemical degradation on NBR/FKM gaskets and copper busbars over 10-yr design life.
Slide 8 | BMS Safety & Firmware

BMS Safety Architecture: Critical Overrides Prohibited (Section 2 & 4)

ABS BMS Requirements (Section 2/5)

  • Critical Overrides Prohibited: No manual or software bypass permitted for critical safety shutdowns (high voltage, overtemp, short circuit).
  • Single-Cell Monitoring: Individual monitoring of every cell/cell-block voltage and representative temperatures.
  • Loss of Comms / Sensor: Reverts battery immediately to a fail-safe de-energized condition.
  • Physical vs. CAN Status: A CAN status message indicating "safe" does not establish that a physical isolation device opened; physical feedback required.

Clean Electric BMS Implementation

  • Cell Tab Sensing: 100% cell voltage coverage; 6 thermistors across immersion pack feeding BMS.
  • Firmware Lockout: Critical safety threshold tripping is hardcoded in non-volatile flash; zero maintenance override.
  • Heartbeat Watchdog: CAN communication timeout (>250ms) prompts graceful shutdown.
  • Dual Isolation: Positive and negative DC contactors backed by series high-breaking pyro-fuses.
IRS 2026 BMS Amendments (from Section 4)
The 2026 BMS amendments identify voltage imbalance, low insulation, cooling failure, and communication failure alarms. Applicable for certification applications dated from 1 July 2026.
Slide 9 | Environmental Qualification

Marine Environmental & Control Qualification: IACS UR E10 (Section 6)

UR E10 (Rev.10, Aug 2024) Test Scope

  • Power Supply Variations: DC ±20% steady-state, transient drops to 0V for 5ms.
  • Dry Heat: +70°C for 16 hours operational.
  • Damp Heat: +55°C at 95% RH (cyclic, 2 cycles of 24 hours).
  • Cold Test: -25°C for 2 hours operational.
  • Sinusoidal Vibration: 2.0 to 13.2 Hz (±1.0 mm); 13.2 to 100 Hz (0.7g acceleration) on 3 orthogonal axes.
  • Inclination: Static roll/pitch 22.5°; dynamic roll 22.5° (10s period).
  • Salt Mist: 4 cycles of 7 days (28 days total) marine corrosion test.
  • EMC Immunity & Emissions: Radiated/conducted emissions, burst, surge, and ESD.

Evaluation Strategy (from Section 6)

  • Operating-Envelope Chart: Separate the cells' allowable operating range, the rack's qualified ambient range, the BMS electronics' test conditions, and cooling capacity. Build a single operating-envelope chart showing which limit constrains BESS at each temperature and SOC.
  • Composite System Distinction: Testing a composite system does not itself generate separate certificates for individual components.
  • Accredited Lab Execution: Perform UR E10 Rev.10 tests once with dual-class witnessing (IRS + ABS) to satisfy both approval files simultaneously.
Slide 10 | Electrical Protection

Electrical Protection: Fault Current, Pre-charge & Isolation (Section 7)

Isolation vs. Containment Argument

  • Stored Electrochemical Energy: Opening a DC contactor removes external current, but cannot remove energy already stored inside each cell.
  • Independent Protection Systems: Separate electrical-isolation argument from heat/gas-containment argument in design review.
  • Prospective Fault Current ($I_{sc}$): Rigorous DC short-circuit calculation per IEC 61660-1 based on LFP cell internal resistance.
  • High-Speed Protection: Semiconductor DC pyro-fuses (>20 kA breaking capacity) clear dead shorts before contactor welding.

Pre-Charge & Insulation Monitoring

  • Pre-charge Resistor Circuit: Limits capacitor inrush current to <1.0C during inverter connection; interlocked with bus voltage (>95%).
  • Continuous Insulation Monitoring: Active ground fault detector on ungrounded (IT) DC bus.
    • Pre-warning Alarm: Set at 100 Ω/V.
    • Critical Trip: Set at 50 Ω/V with automatic system isolation.
Slide 11 | Enclosure & Ingress

Mechanical Ingress Protection & Pressure Relief System

Ingress Protection (IP Rating)

  • ABS Requirement (Sec 2/2.2): Minimum IP44 in dedicated battery spaces; minimum IP67 on exposed weather decks.
  • IRS Requirement (INST-04): Minimum IP44 recommended.
  • VFCPL Baseline: High-integrity sealed aluminum casing rated to IP67 throughout. Exceeds standard battery room requirements.

Expansion & Thermal Breathing

  • Fluid Volumetric Expansion: Dielectric fluid expands with temperature changes (β ≈ 0.0007 /°C).
  • Internal Headspace: Inert gas headspace or internal expansion bellows accommodates fluid expansion across -10°C to +65°C without casing overpressure.

Overpressure Relief (PRV)

  • Pressure Relief Valve: Spring-loaded PRV or rupture disc sized to discharge off-gas during thermal runaway.
  • Venting Duct Interface: PRV discharge port connects directly to vessel external exhaust duct, discharging safely to open air away from ignition sources.

Slide 13 | Fire & Gas Safety

Fire Detection, Gas Monitoring & Water-Based Extinguishing

Gas & Fire Detection Architecture

  • Smoke Detection: Addressable optical smoke detectors compliant with IMO FSS Code.
  • Off-Gas / LFL Monitoring: Flammable gas detectors monitoring $H_2$ / hydrocarbons:
    • At 30% LFL: Audio-visual alarm at manned control station, automatic start of 6 ACH emergency ventilation, and BMS trip.
    • At 60% LFL: Total automatic de-energization of all non-Ex electrical circuits in the compartment.
  • Thermal Imaging: Fixed thermographic / IR cameras recommended by IRS GL Rev.2 §2.6.4.2 for lithium installations.

Fixed Extinguishing System Requirements

  • Water-Based Mandatory: Fixed water mist or sprinkler system approved for Category A machinery spaces (IMO MSC.1/Circ.1165).
  • Cooling Effectiveness: Class rules prohibit gaseous systems ($CO_2$, FM-200) as primary lithium runaway protection due to lack of cooling capacity.
  • Immersion Protection Synergy: Dielectric fluid quenches internal cell runaway; external water mist prevents structural bulkheads from overheating and suppresses room fires.

Slide 15 | AMBESS Operational Concept

AMBESS Operational Concept: Hotel Power & Essential Service Segregation

Anchor Mode Operating Philosophy

  • Duty Profile: Supplies vessel hotel and auxiliary electrical loads during anchor watch, shutting down diesel generators.
  • Recharge Cycle: Generators operate in short, high-efficiency blocks (>75% MCR) to recharge BESS, avoiding low-load wet-stacking.
  • Solar Offset: Integrated DC/DC solar array offsets auxiliary charging demand in port/anchorage.
  • Thermal Cycling: Validated over 818 cycles with 89.65% capacity retention and minimal cell degradation.

Essential Services & Redundancy Rules

  • ABS & IRS Rule Alignment: If BESS powers main propulsion, dual redundant battery rooms and split distribution are mandatory.
  • AMBESS Distinction: AMBESS is classed as Auxiliary Power / Propulsion Support.
  • Electrical Interlock: A trip or short-circuit on the BESS DC bus must never backfeed or trip primary shipboard diesel generators or blackout steering gear.

Slide 18 | Submission Dossier Structure

Controlled Submission Folders & 4-Tier Evidence States (Section 11)

The 8 Controlled Submission Folders

  • 1. Basis: Intended use (AMBESS), exclusions, rule editions, model family, approval strategy.
  • 2. Design: Electrical SLD, mechanical drawings, BOM, operating envelope chart.
  • 3. Safety: Hazard register, FMEA, protective trip matrix, unresolved assumptions.
  • 4. Controls: BMS/converter interface, alarm list, firmware ID, software quality plan.
  • 5. Thermal: Immersion cooling technical submission, heat model, fluid spec, compatibility.
  • 6. Testing: Test procedures, class comments, witness records, reports, deviation logs.
  • 7. Manufacturing: Factory quality plan, cell batch traceability, assembly QA, change controls.
  • 8. Delivery: Unit identification, O&M manual, installation limits, FAT test records.

The 4-Tier Evidence States (Section 11)

Avoid a single "compliant" column: a design intention, a test result, and class acceptance are different levels of evidence.

  • ACCEPTED Formally approved in writing by the classification society surveyor / plan review office.
  • SUBMITTED Formally delivered to class; currently pending technical review.
  • GAP Requirement identified but evidence or test report does not yet exist; action required.
  • NOT APPLICABLE Excluded with formal engineering justification approved by class.
