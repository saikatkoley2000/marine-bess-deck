Certification Pathways: What Certificate Should a BESS Supplier Seek? (Section 3)

1. Product Design (PDA)

Technical review of defined product against stated rules. 5-year validity. Covers drawings, cell test reports (IEC 62619/62620), and thermal runaway data (UL 9540A).

2. Manufacturing (MA)

Assessment of production and quality processes at Clean Electric and VFCPL. Requires ISO 9001 basis and annual surveillance audits.

3. Unit Certification

Surveyor witness testing of the physical delivered ship units at the factory. Generic product assessment (PDA) does not settle every installation condition.

OEM vs. Secondary Manufacturing Distinction (from Section 3): Clean Electric manufactures cells/BMS/modules, while VFCPL carries out pack integration in India. ABS application forms require explicit identification of OEM design ownership and separate/duplicate PDA arrangements for secondary manufacturing sites.

Thermal-Runaway Evidence: The 7 Review Questions (Section 8)

The 7 Review Questions (Section 8)

  1. Does tested cell have exactly the same construction as purchased cell?
  2. Does report identify SOC (100%), conditioning, initiating method, and orientation?
  3. Was tested assembly representative of the most challenging offered arrangement?
  4. Were cooling and active protective systems running, failed, or unavailable?
  5. What happened to adjacent cells/modules, enclosure pressure, and gas release?
  6. Are measured gas/heat data sufficient for integrator's ventilation analysis?
  7. Which changes in spacing, barriers, enclosure, or coolant invalidate inference?

Non-Propagation Physics

ABS Section 2/2.1 mandates preventing cell-to-cell propagation, or at minimum module-to-module containment with zero external flaming and casing temperature <100°C.

VFCPL Immersion Advantage: Dielectric fluid provides direct sensible and latent heat absorption, rapidly dispersing localized thermal energy before adjacent cells reach decomposition temperature ($T_c$).

Dielectric Coolant Fluid Properties & Certifications

Electrical Isolation

Dielectric strength >50 kV (IEC 60156); volume resistivity >1012 Ω·m at 25°C. Prevents live busbar arcing up to 1000 VDC under dynamic vessel motion.

Thermal & Fire

Flash point >160°C (ISO 2719), Fire point >190°C (ISO 2592), Auto-ignition >350°C. Viscosity <35 mm²/s at 40°C; pumping viable at -20°C.

Environmental & Marine

Readily biodegradable (>60% in 28d, OECD 301B); non-toxic to marine organisms (OECD 201/202/203). 10-year gasket compatibility.

BMS Safety Architecture: Critical Overrides Prohibited (Section 2 & 4)

ABS BMS Rules (Section 2/5)

  • Overrides Forbidden: No manual override or software bypass permitted for over-V, under-V, over-T, or short-circuit shutdowns.
  • Physical vs. CAN Status: A CAN status message indicating "safe" does NOT establish that a contactor opened; physical feedback verification required.
  • Loss of Comms: Timeout (>250ms) triggers immediate fail-safe isolation.

Clean Electric Implementation

  • 100% cell-tab voltage coverage; 6 thermistors across pack feeding BMS.
  • Firmware permanently locks out manual override for high-voltage, cell runaway, and short circuit.
  • Dual positive/negative DC contactors backed by series high-breaking pyro-fuses.

Electrical Protection: Fault Current, Pre-charge & Isolation (Section 7)

Isolation vs. Containment Argument

Opening a DC contactor removes external current flow, but cannot remove energy already stored inside electrochemical cells. Treat electrical isolation and thermal/gas containment as separate protective systems.

Selective Protection Hierarchy

  • Semiconductor DC pyro-fuses (>20 kA breaking) for sub-millisecond dead short clearance.
  • Pre-charge resistor limits inrush current to <1.0C during inverter connection (>95% VDC).
  • Continuous ungrounded IT bus insulation monitoring (100 Ω/V warning, 50 Ω/V trip).

Mechanical Enclosure, Ingress Protection & Pressure Relief

Ingress Protection

ABS Section 2/2.2 requires min IP44 in battery spaces and IP67 on open deck. VFCPL monolithic casing is certified to IP67 throughout.

Thermal Breathing

Internal expansion headspace cushion or bellows accommodates fluid expansion across -10°C to +65°C without casing deformation.

Pressure Relief (PRV)

Spring-loaded PRV or rupture disc sized for runaway off-gas flow; flanged for exterior ducting to weather deck.

Fire Detection, Gas Monitoring & Water-Based Extinguishing

Fire & Gas Detection Logic

  • Optical smoke detectors complying with IMO FSS Code.
  • Flammable gas detectors: at 30% LFL triggers audio-visual alarm, 6 ACH emergency exhaust, and BMS trip.
  • At 60% LFL triggers total automatic de-energization of all non-Ex circuits in the compartment.
  • Fixed thermographic / IR cameras recommended for lithium installations.

Fixed Extinguishing Medium

  • Mandatory fixed water-based system (water mist / sprinkler) approved for Category A spaces (IMO MSC.1/Circ.1165).
  • Class rejects purely gaseous systems (CO2, FM-200) as primary lithium protection due to lack of cooling capacity.
  • Immersion fluid quenches internal cell heat; external water mist keeps casing and ship bulkheads cool.

Structural & CSC

A-60 Envelope

AMBESS Operational Concept: Hotel Power & Essential Services

Anchor Mode Operating Philosophy

  • Supplies vessel hotel and auxiliary electrical loads during anchor watch, shutting down diesel generators.
  • Generators operate in short, high-efficiency blocks (>75% MCR) to recharge BESS, avoiding low-load wet-stacking.
  • Integrated DC/DC solar array offsets auxiliary charging demand in port/anchorage.
  • Validated over 818 cycles with 89.65% capacity retention and minimal cell degradation.

Essential Service Segregation

  • Classed as Auxiliary Power / Propulsion Support (not main propulsion).
  • A fault, trip, or short-circuit on BESS DC bus must NEVER cause blackout or tripping of primary diesel generators or steering gear.
  • Exempt from mandatory dual redundant battery rooms and split propulsion switchboards.

The 4-Tier Evidence States

Avoid a single "compliant" column: design intention, test results, and class acceptance are different levels of evidence.

  • ACCEPTED Formally approved in writing by class surveyor or plan approval office.
  • SUBMITTED Formally delivered to class; currently pending technical review.
  • GAP Requirement identified but evidence does not yet exist; action required.
  • NOT APPLICABLE Excluded with formal engineering justification approved by class.

7. BMS Software Assessment: Agree on Software Quality Plan (SQP) review, override lockout check, and fault simulation scope.
8. Unit Certification Hold Points: Define factory FAT hold points requiring physical surveyor attendance vs. calibration reviews.
9. Installation Certificate Limits: Agree on wording of external shipyard installation dependencies (A-60, ventilation, water mist).
10. Change Notification Rules: Establish formal Engineering Change Order (ECO) thresholds for cells, firmware, and casing.
